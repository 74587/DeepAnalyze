# Palmer Penguins 演示问题 / Demo Questions

上传并选择 `penguins.csv`。 / Upload and select `penguins.csv`.

## 完整体征分析

请分析这份企鹅观测数据。先说明数据规模、字段含义和缺失值情况，再回答：

1. 三个物种在体重、鳍长、喙长和喙深上有哪些明显差异？
2. 在每个物种内部，雌雄企鹅的体重差异是否一致？请给出样本数、均值和中位数，避免只看总体平均数。
3. 鳍长与体重的关系有多强？这种关系是否可能只是物种差异造成的？

请生成一张“鳍长-体重”散点图（按物种着色、按性别区分形状）和一张按物种分组的体重箱线图，最后用 3 条通俗结论总结，并说明缺失值如何处理。将图表和清洗后的摘要表保存为文件。

## Full trait analysis

Analyze this penguin observation dataset. Start by describing its size, field meanings, and missing values, then answer:

1. What clear differences do the three species show in body mass, flipper length, bill length, and bill depth?
2. Within each species, is the body-mass difference between females and males consistent? Report sample size, mean, and median instead of relying only on an overall average.
3. How strong is the relationship between flipper length and body mass? Could that relationship be explained mainly by differences between species?

Create a flipper-length versus body-mass scatter plot colored by species and shaped by sex, plus a species-grouped body-mass box plot. Finish with three plain-language findings, explain how missing values were handled, and save the charts and cleaned summary table as files.

## 快速区分物种 / Quick species comparison

中文：用一张图和不超过 5 句话告诉我：三种企鹅最容易通过哪些体征区分？结论必须带具体数值。

English: Using one chart and no more than five sentences, explain which traits most clearly distinguish the three penguin species. Every conclusion must include specific numbers.
