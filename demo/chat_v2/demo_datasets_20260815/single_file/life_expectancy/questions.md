# 全球预期寿命演示问题 / Global Life Expectancy Demo Questions

上传并选择 `life_expectancy.csv` 和 `source_metadata.json`。 / Upload and select `life_expectancy.csv` and `source_metadata.json`.

## 1. 长期提升排名 / Long-run improvement ranking

中文：只使用这六国在 year=1950 和 year=2023 的记录，计算 2023 减 1950 的预期寿命差值并排序；哪个国家提升最多？

English: Use only the records with year=1950 and year=2023 for these six countries. Calculate 2023 minus 1950, rank the gains, and identify the country with the largest increase.

## 2. 显著下降年份 / Largest decline years

中文：在每个国家的完整时间范围内，计算相邻年份的预期寿命差值（当年减前一年），分别列出每国降幅最大的一年，并指出六国中最大的一次下降。

English: Across the complete time range available for each country, calculate year-over-year differences (current year minus previous year). Report the year with the largest drop for each country and identify the largest drop among all six.

## 3. 疫情后恢复 / Post-pandemic recovery

中文：从 2019 年到 2023 年，中国、美国、印度、日本、巴西和南非的预期寿命如何变化？到 2023 年，哪些国家已经回到或超过 2019 年水平？

English: How did life expectancy change from 2019 to 2023 in China, the United States, India, Japan, Brazil, and South Africa, and which countries had returned to or exceeded their 2019 level by 2023?
