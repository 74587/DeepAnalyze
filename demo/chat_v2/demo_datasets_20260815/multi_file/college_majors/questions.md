# College Majors 演示问题 / Demo Questions

上传并选择 `recent-grads.csv`、`all-ages.csv`、`grad-students.csv` 和 `majors-list.csv`。 / Upload and select `recent-grads.csv`, `all-ages.csv`, `grad-students.csv`, and `majors-list.csv`.

## 1. 多表关联质量 / Cross-file join quality

中文：各文件中的专业代码、专业名和类别能否正确对应？请列出无法关联或存在冲突的记录，并保存核对表。

English: Do major codes, names, and categories align correctly across the files? List any unmatched or conflicting records and save the reconciliation table.

## 2. 专业类别就业表现 / Outcomes by major category

中文：近期毕业生中，哪些专业类别的收入较高且失业率较低？请报告各类别的专业数和样本规模，并用一张图比较。

English: Among recent graduates, which major categories combine higher earnings with lower unemployment? Report the number of majors and sample size per category and compare them in one chart.

## 3. 不同年龄口径收入差异 / Earnings across age groups

中文：同一专业在 `recent-grads.csv` 与 `all-ages.csv` 中的收入中位数差距有多大？请列出差距最大的 10 个专业，并说明这不是个人薪资增长。

English: How large is the median-earnings gap for the same major between `recent-grads.csv` and `all-ages.csv`? List the ten largest gaps and explain that this is not an individual's salary growth.

## 4. 研究生收入溢价 / Graduate earnings premium

中文：哪些专业的研究生收入溢价较高，同时研究生失业率没有明显恶化？请给出一个透明筛选标准和前 10 名表格。

English: Which majors have a high graduate earnings premium without clearly worse graduate unemployment? Define a transparent screening rule and provide a top-ten table.

## 5. 女性占比与收入 / Women's share and earnings

中文：近期毕业生中，女性占比与收入中位数有什么关系？请用散点图展示，并说明按专业类别比较后关系是否仍然明显，不要把相关性表述为因果。

English: Among recent graduates, how is women's share related to median earnings? Show the relationship in a scatter plot and explain whether it remains clear within major categories, without presenting correlation as causation.

## 6. 高收入低失业专业 / High-earning, low-unemployment majors

中文：近期毕业生中，哪些专业呈现“收入较高且失业率较低”？请给出透明筛选标准、前 10 名表格和一张散点图。

English: Among recent graduates, which majors combine relatively high earnings with low unemployment? Define a transparent screening rule and provide a top-ten table and one scatter plot.
