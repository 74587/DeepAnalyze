## 完整专业回报分析

请联合分析美国大学专业与就业收入数据：

1. 核对各文件中的专业代码、专业名和类别能否正确对应，列出缺失或冲突记录。
2. 对近期毕业生，按专业类别汇总收入中位数、失业率和低薪工作占比；每项给出表现最好和最差的类别，并报告专业数与样本规模。
3. 比较相同专业在 `recent-grads.csv` 与 `all-ages.csv` 中的收入中位数。哪些专业的差距最大？说明这只是不同年龄口径的横截面对比，不能直接当成个人薪资增长。
4. 结合 `grad-students.csv`，找出研究生收入溢价较高、同时研究生失业率没有明显恶化的专业或类别。
5. 分析女性占比与近期毕业生收入中位数的相关关系；控制专业类别后，方向是否仍一致？不要把相关性表述为因果。

请生成一张类别对比表、两张清晰图表，并给出 5 条面向选专业者的通俗结论。所有结论必须标明使用的是哪一种人群口径，并注明数据来自 2010-2012 年美国调查。将图表保存为文件。

## Full major-outcomes analysis

Analyze the U.S. college-major employment and earnings data across files:

1. Verify that major codes, names, and categories align across the files, and list any missing or conflicting records.
2. For recent graduates, summarize median earnings, unemployment rate, and low-wage-job share by major category. For each metric, identify the best and worst categories and report the number of majors and sample size.
3. Compare median earnings for the same major in `recent-grads.csv` and `all-ages.csv`. Which majors have the largest gaps? Explain that this is a cross-sectional comparison of different age groups, not an individual's salary growth.
4. Use `grad-students.csv` to identify majors or categories with a high graduate-degree earnings premium and no clear deterioration in graduate unemployment.
5. Analyze the relationship between women's share and median earnings among recent graduates. Does the direction remain after accounting for major category? Do not present correlation as causation.

Create one category comparison table and two clear charts, then provide five plain-language findings for people choosing a major. Every finding must state which population definition it uses and note that the data comes from the 2010-2012 U.S. survey. Save the charts and tables as files.
